📋 About the Test

The Leben in Deutschland test (and Einbürgerungstest) consists of 310 questions total — 300 national + 10 state-specific. On the actual exam you answer 33 questions in 60 minutes. You need 17 correct (about 50%) to pass. Questions cover German democracy, law, society, history, and daily life.
